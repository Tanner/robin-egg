# Recording script

Each person makes two Voice Memos: one with the "Hey Robin" list, one with the don't-wake list. That's four files:

- `tanner_positive.m4a`, `ryan_positive.m4a`
- `tanner_negative.m4a`, `ryan_negative.m4a`

Before you start:

- In Settings → Apps → Voice Memos, set Audio Quality to **Lossless**. Turn off **Enhance Recording** in the memo.
- Leave about 2 seconds of quiet between takes. The notebook cuts the memo at the pauses.
- Don't say anything else in the memo, not even "next" or a laugh you can hear. Anything said between takes becomes a take. If you flub one, stay quiet for a few seconds and go on. A bad cut can be deleted later.
- Read the line to yourself, then say the phrase. Don't read the instructions aloud.
- Record alone. If the other person talks in the background, their words get cut out and labelled as takes.
- Say only "Hey Robin" in the first memo, with nothing after it. Words after it turn into takes of their own, or teach the model the wrong ending.

## "Hey Robin" (about 40 takes)

Say "Hey Robin" each time, the way the line describes.

**Near the phone, about an arm's length away**

1. Normal voice
2. Normal voice again
3. Quietly
4. Whispering
5. Loudly
6. Quickly
7. Slowly
8. Sleepy, as if you just woke up
9. Cheerful
10. Tired or annoyed
11. As a question: "Hey Robin?"
12. Mumbled
13. With a long "Heyyy Robin"
14. With a short "Hey" and a stretched "Robiiin"
15. Leaning toward the phone, then away mid-phrase
16. While smiling or laughing a little

**Across the room**

17. Normal voice
18. Normal voice again
19. Loudly
20. Quietly
21. Facing away from the phone
22. Facing away, loudly
23. While walking across the room
24. While sitting on the couch or in a chair
25. With your hand near your mouth, as if you're eating
26. Quickly, in passing

**From the next room, door open**

27. Normal voice
28. Loudly
29. Calling out, as if the egg is far away
30. Facing away from the doorway

**With noise in the room**

31. With the TV or music on at normal volume
32. With the TV or music on, loudly
33. With the TV or music on, from across the room
34. With the tap running or the dishwasher on
35. With the TV or music on, facing away

**Anything goes**

36. Your most natural way of saying it
37. Your most natural way, again
38. The way you'd say it first thing in the morning
39. The way you'd say it from bed
40. However you like

## Don't wake (about 20 takes)

Say each line as written, in a normal voice from wherever you're sitting. These are the sounds and phrases most likely to set the egg off by mistake.

1. Hey Ryan
2. Hey Ryan, come here
3. Robin
4. Robin?
5. Hey Rob
6. Hey Robert
7. Hey Rowan
8. Hey Robbie
9. Hey Siri
10. Hey, are you hungry?
11. Hey there
12. Robbing
13. They're robbing the place
14. Robin said the package came
15. I saw a robin in the yard
16. Ask Robin about it later
17. That's robin's egg blue
18. Ryan, did you feed Clara?
19. Hey, Ryan and Robin are coming over
20. Have you seen the remote?

Don't add lines that contain "hey robin" anywhere, such as "Hey, Robin Hood is on." The model would learn that the wake word itself means "don't wake."
