## wake_word_triggered.flac

American robin (*Turdus migratorius*) call, recorded by Jonathon Jongsma, xeno-canto XC254483. Licensed under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Source: [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Turdus_migratorius_-_American_Robin_XC254483.mp3).

Changes: cut a single call (0.28 s, from 3.65 s in the original), high-passed at 1 kHz, denoised, faded in and out, peak-normalized to -1 dBFS, and converted to 48 kHz mono 16-bit FLAC. This file is shared under the same license.
